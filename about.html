Heritage & Craftsmanship

"We Create the New Fashion in the Bag Industry."

From our flagship boutique along Galle Road in Mount Lavinia to fashion enthusiasts across Sri Lanka — CLARA was founded on the belief that a woman's handbag is her most intimate architectural staple.

Our Philosophy

Sculptural Form That Retains Its Grace

Every CLARA silhouette begins with structural engineering. We select high-density vegan leathers, including rigid Palmellato, textured Saffiano, and supple pebbled finishes, calibrated to resist moisture, daily friction, and weather conditions while maintaining crisp architectural silhouettes.

Whether engineered for an executive carrying her laptop through Colombo or a bride celebrating in an evening gown, our bags are designed to make a quiet yet unmistakable statement of refinement.
