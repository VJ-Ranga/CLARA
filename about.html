Made for her

CLARA began with a simple belief: a great handbag is the one piece every woman reaches for, every single day. So we set out to create bags that feel special — and are made to last.

Our philosophy

Form that keeps its grace

Every CLARA silhouette starts with structure. We choose dense vegan leathers — firm Palmellato, textured Saffiano and soft pebbled finishes — that resist humidity, daily wear and Colombo's busy streets, while keeping their crisp, beautiful lines.

Whether she's carrying a laptop to the office or a jewelled clutch to a wedding, our bags are designed to make a quiet, unmistakable statement — never loud, always elegant.
