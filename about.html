Every CLARA silhouette starts with structure. We choose dense vegan leathers — firm Palmellato, textured Saffiano and soft pebbled finishes — that resist humidity and daily wear while keeping their crisp, beautiful lines.

Whether she carries a laptop to the office or a jewelled clutch to a wedding, our bags make a quiet, unmistakable statement: never loud, always elegant.
