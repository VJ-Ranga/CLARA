Privacy Policy

What information we collect, why we need it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: November 2026

This website is run by CLARA Bags (DJ Industries), No. 587/A, Galle Road, Mount Lavinia, Sri Lanka. This policy explains how we handle your personal information.

Information we collect

  • Order details: your name, email, phone number, delivery address, delivery notes and what you bought.
  • Messages: what you send us through our contact form, WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • Newsletter: your email address, if you subscribe.
  • Payment: how you chose to pay. Bank slips you send us are kept with your order.

How we use it

  • To process, confirm and deliver your order
  • To answer your questions and handle exchanges
  • To send news and offers, only if you subscribed
  • To keep the records the law requires

Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We share only what is needed to complete your order, with couriers who deliver it and payment providers who process it, and with authorities where the law requires it.

Information saved in your browser

Your shopping bag, wishlist, recently viewed items and latest order are saved in your own browser, so they are still there when you come back. You can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires.

Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you
  • Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time by contacting us

Contact us

For any privacy question or request, email clara2bags@gmail.com or call 077 317 3217.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
