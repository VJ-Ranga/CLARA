Delivery & Returns

How your bag reaches you, and what to do if it isn't quite right.

Delivery options

MethodTimeCharge
Colombo & Western Province1–2 business daysRs. 350
Islandwide courier2–4 business daysRs. 550

Delivery is free on orders over Rs. 10,000, anywhere in Sri Lanka.

After you order

  • Our team contacts you on the phone number you gave us to confirm dispatch.
  • For cash on delivery, the courier calls before arriving. Please have the full amount ready.
  • For bank transfer, please pay within 24 hours using your order number as the reference, then send the slip on WhatsApp to 077 317 3217.
  • You can check on an order any time on our Track Order page.

7-day inspection & exchange guarantee

Inspect your bag at home. If it isn't right, contact us within 7 days of receiving it and we'll exchange it.

To qualify, the bag must be:

  • Unused, and in the same condition you received it
  • Returned with its original tags and packaging
  • Accompanied by your order number or receipt

Faulty or wrong item

If your bag arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, contact us as soon as possible with your order number and a photo. We'll put it right at no cost to you.

How to request an exchange

  1. Message us on WhatsApp or call 077 317 3217 with your order number.
  2. Tell us what you would like to exchange it for.
  3. We'll arrange a courier to collect the bag and deliver your exchange.
